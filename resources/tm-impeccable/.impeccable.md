## Design Context

### Users
Dutch consumers buying high-ticket **garden/outdoor structures** (verandas, garden rooms,
canopies, carports). Considered, multi-session, risk-aware purchases on desktop and mobile.
Full detail in [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md).

### Brand Personality
**Trustworthy · solid · helpful** — a **product surface** where design serves the shopping
task. Authority through clarity and proof, not decoration. Emotional goal at decision points:
**confidence.** References: Coolblue, Hornbach, IKEA (trust through clarity and organization).

### Aesthetic Direction
Warm, light, dependable commerce: sand/beige surfaces, deep-green structure, rare orange/lime
accents. Light theme always. Anti-refs: AI-slop, off-brand colors/fonts, dark/heavy layouts,
loud sale styling. Tokens and components in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).

### Design Principles
1. Confidence over persuasion. 2. Organized = trustworthy. 3. Green structures, orange acts.
4. Warm, light, roomy. 5. Token discipline + WCAG 2.2 AA. (Full text in PRODUCT.md.)
